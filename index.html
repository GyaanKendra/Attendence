Employee Attendance System Guide

Step 1: Create an Account

Click on the "Sign Up" tab and fill in all your details including full name, email, username, mobile number, and daily shift target.

Step 2: Mark Attendance

Click "Check In" when starting work and "Check Out" upon departure. Click "Absent" or "Holiday" when taking leave.

Step 3: Add Overtime Remarks

Click the '+' or 'Note' button next to any date to add a description for extra duty or leaves.

Step 4: View Previous Months

Use the 'Previous' and 'Next' buttons above the cards to inspect past attendance and download past reports.

Step 5: View Analytics & PDF

Click "View Attendance Analytics" to see detailed logs and download an official PDF copy.
